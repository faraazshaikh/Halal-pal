Available on Android only, for now

Halal Pal is currently an Android app. There is no iPhone version yet.

iPhone users: Apple doesn’t allow installing apps from a downloaded file, so I can’t offer an iPhone version yet. It would need an Apple developer account and Apple’s testing service, TestFlight. If you’d like an iPhone version, email hello@intelisenselabs.com and I’ll tell you when it’s ready. Until then, you can read the ingredient list on the pack yourself.

How to install on Android

  1. Download the file and tap it. If Android asks, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  2. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. It appears because the app isn’t on the Play Store.
  3. Open Halal Pal and allow the camera.

Every time you press Download you get the newest version. To update, download and install it again over the old one; your history stays.

How it works

Point the camera at a product barcode. The verdict shows first:

  • HalalGreen. No haram or doubtful ingredients found.
  • HaramRed. Contains a haram ingredient. The ingredient and the reason are shown.
  • MushboohAmber. Doubtful, depends on the source. The ingredient and reason are shown.
  • No dataGrey. No ingredient list, so check the pack yourself.

Our aim

Halal Pal will keep being developed. Every update aims to make it more accurate and quicker to use, to help Muslim brothers and sisters eat halal with confidence.

Please know

  • It is a helper based on the ingredient list, not a religious ruling or a halal certificate. If you’re unsure, check the pack for a halal mark or ask a scholar.
  • It reads English ingredient lists only for now. Other languages are planned.
  • Found a wrong result? Tap Report an issue with this product in the app, or email hello@intelisenselabs.com.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. Your scan history stays on your phone. To fetch a product, the app sends its barcode to Open Food Facts.
